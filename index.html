Elastic Labs LLC is an independent Florida studio based in Miami. We focus on readable systems, expressive worlds, and cooperative play that creates memorable stories.

Working title · Private Steam Playtest

Giant & Ghosts

One giant. Too many directions.

Each player controls a ghost, while the whole team shares responsibility for one giant. The ghosts do not fight directly: they guide movement, position commands and abilities, carry resources, and work together to keep the giant's flame alive.

Runs move through objective-driven arenas built around coordination, escalating choices, and a final confrontation. A fixed isometric view and controller-oriented command scheme keep the action readable while the team creates its own co-op chaos.

A working Mac development demo is distributed privately to invited testers through Steam Playtest. It remains under active development and is not publicly released.

  • Cooperative roguelite
  • Dark fantasy
  • Controller-oriented
  • Isometric
