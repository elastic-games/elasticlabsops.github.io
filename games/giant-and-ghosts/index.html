Giant & Ghosts

One giant. Too many directions.

A cooperative dark-fantasy roguelite where players become ghosts and share responsibility for one vulnerable giant. Guide his path, direct his powers, carry the resources that sustain him, and work together to keep his flame alive.

  • Cooperative roguelite
  • Dark fantasy
  • Fixed isometric view
  • Controller-oriented
Explore the design Contact Elastic Labs

The central idea

The biggest character needs the whole team.

The giant is the center of every encounter, but the ghosts decide what happens next. They float over the battlefield as commanders, guides, and positional targeting cursors.

The ghosts are not conventional fighters. Their strength comes from coordination: directing the giant, delivering resources, responding to danger, and balancing different ideas about the team’s next move.

Shared responsibility

How it is designed to play

01

Guide through position

A ghost’s location helps define the giant’s next destination and the direction of his actions.

02

Command shared power

Players coordinate movement and ability commands around one shared protagonist.

03

Support the journey

Ghosts gather and deliver resources that sustain the giant and contribute to progression.

04

Protect the flame

The run continues while the giant survives; his flame reaching zero is the central failure condition.

05

Stay readable under pressure

The fixed camera and controller-oriented interaction are designed to keep team choices legible.

Run structure

A systems-driven roguelite loop

Runs are designed around replayable objectives and changing pressure rather than a fixed sequence of missions.

  1. Run
  2. Arena
  3. Objective
  4. Modifier
  5. Pressure
  6. Payoff

Destroy

Guide the giant toward priority targets and coordinate his attacks.

Hold

Keep the giant inside an active area while pressure builds.

Collect & deliver

Use ghost mobility to recover objective items and move them into place.

Hunt

Direct the giant toward a moving target that tries to preserve its distance.

Survive

Keep the giant alive through escalating pressure until the scenario ends.
